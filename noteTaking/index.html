Unlock Your Notes

Enter your password to decrypt and view your notes.

No password recovery. Your notes are encrypted in this browser with a key derived from your password (AES-256, PBKDF2). If you forget it, there is no way to recover your notes — not even I can reset it for you. Consider writing it down somewhere safe.
Browser-only storage. Notes live only in this browser's local storage — they won't sync to other devices, and clearing site data will erase them. Use "Export" once unlocked to keep an unencrypted backup file.
Truly forgotten your password? There is deliberately no "reset" button here — a one-click wipe next to the password box would let anyone with a moment of access to this device destroy your notes without knowing the password. If you genuinely need to start over: open your browser's DevTools console on this page and run localStorage.removeItem('noteTaking_vault_v1'), then reload.
